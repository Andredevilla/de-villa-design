# Bloom Yoga Studio — Business Information
*(Fictional demo business created by De Villa Design as concept portfolio work.)*

## Identity
- **Name:** Bloom Yoga Studio
- **Tagline (header):** Breathe in. Bloom out.
- **Subheader:** A sun-drenched Byron Bay studio for every body — from your first downward dog to your thousandth.
- **Voice:** Warm, welcoming, a little poetic. Zero gatekeeping; beginners belong.

## Services / Classes
1. **Vinyasa Flow** — breath-led movement, all levels (60 min)
2. **Yin & Restore** — slow, deep stretch with props and stillness (75 min)
3. **Beginners' Foundations** — 4-week intro course, small groups
4. **Hot 26** — heated classic sequence (60 min)
5. **Prenatal Yoga** — supported practice for every trimester
6. **Sunrise Beach Sessions** — Saturdays at Wategos Beach, weather permitting

**Pricing:** Casual class $24 · 10-pack $199 · Unlimited monthly $159 · First week free for locals

## Theme & Design

- **Imagery:** Golden-hour light, natural textures, real practice moments
