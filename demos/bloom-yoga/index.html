What We Offer

Find your practice

Six styles. Seven teachers. One community that holds space for wherever you are.

Vinyasa Flow

60 min

Breath-led movement that links posture to posture in a seamless, energising sequence. Suitable for all levels.

Yin & Restore

75 min

Slow, deep stretching held for longer to release connective tissue. Props, silence and full permission to do less.

Beginners' Foundations

4-week course

A small-group intro course designed to build confidence, alignment knowledge, and a language for your body. Start any month.

Hot 26

60 min

The classic 26-posture sequence in a heated room. Detoxifying, challenging and deeply meditative. Towel and water essential.

Prenatal Yoga

All trimesters

A supported, nurturing practice tailored to pregnancy. Breathwork, gentle movement and community for every stage of the journey.

Sunrise Beach Sessions

Saturdays

Catch the golden hour at Wategos Beach. Weather-permitting outdoor classes — bring a mat, sand optional, joy mandatory.

Our Home

A space that breathes with you

Bloom was born from a simple belief: yoga should feel like coming home. Warm, unhurried, and designed to melt the day away the moment you walk in.

✦

7 certified teachers — 200–500hr YTT trained, with specialisations across styles.

✦

Two beautiful rooms — the Garden Room (ground floor, leafy outlook) and the Light Room (upstairs, north-facing).

✦

Everything you need — quality mats, blocks, bolsters, straps and blankets all provided.

✦

Clean showers — so you can practice before work without the guilt.

✦

Monthly full-moon gatherings — community circles, cacao and candlelight.
