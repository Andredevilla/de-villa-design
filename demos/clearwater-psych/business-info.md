## Identity
- **Name:** Clearwater Psychology
- **Tagline:** A steadier place to think.
- **Voice:** Warm, calm, de-stigmatising. Plain language, no jargon, no judgement. Human, not clinical.

## About
A warm, private practice for adults and teens navigating anxiety, low mood, burnout, grief and life transitions. No waiting-room awkwardness — soft lighting, tea, and clinicians who listen before they advise. In person in North Adelaide or by secure video anywhere in SA.

Medicare rebates available with a Mental Health Care Plan from your GP (up to 10 rebated sessions/yr) · most private health funds · NDIS (plan & self-managed) · LGBTQ+ affirming.

## Theme & Design
- **Aesthetic:** Warm, editorial, reassuring. Soft bone background, sage/forest greens, generous whitespace, arched hero image, rounded cards. Subtle "rise" entrance animation.
- **Palette:** Bone `#F6F3EE` (background), forest green `#41503D` (primary), sage `#6E7F6A`, dark `#23211D` (services section), gold `#C99A4E` (stars), soft band `#EDEFE8`.
- **Typography:** Newsreader (serif display/headings) + Hanken Grotesk (sans body).
- **Imagery:** Serene client by a sunlit window (hero), cosy clinic interior, three practitioner headshots, North Adelaide streetscape (contact).
- **Note:** The source design offered two hero layouts via a switcher (Split / Centered). This build ships the default **Split** hero.
