About Clearwater

Therapy that feels human, not clinical.

Clearwater is a warm, private practice for adults and teens navigating anxiety, low mood, burnout, grief and life transitions. There's no waiting-room awkwardness — clinicians who listen before they advise, in plain language and at your pace.

In person in North Adelaide, or by secure video anywhere in South Australia.
