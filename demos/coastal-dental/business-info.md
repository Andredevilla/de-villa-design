# Coastal Dental — Business Information
*(Fictional demo business created by De Villa Design as concept portfolio work.)*

## Identity
- **Name:** Coastal Dental
- **Tagline (header):** Smiles worth the sunshine.
- **Subheader:** Gentle, modern dentistry on the Gold Coast — honest advice, fair pricing, and a team that actually listens.
- **Voice:** Fresh, friendly, trustworthy. Anti-scary-dentist. Transparent about cost.

## Services
1. **Check-up & clean** — exam, scale, polish and X-rays ($199 new-patient offer)
2. **Cosmetic dentistry** — whitening, veneers and smile makeovers
3. **Invisalign®** — discreet teeth straightening with 3D preview
4. **Dental implants** — single tooth to full-arch restoration
5. **Children's dentistry** — gentle care, bulk-billed under Medicare CDBS
6. **Emergency appointments** — same-day care for pain and accidents

## Theme & Design
- **Aesthetic:** Coastal-fresh and clinical-clean. Crisp white, ocean accents, lots of light, friendly rounded UI.
- **Colours:** Ocean teal `#0E7490` (primary), seafoam `#E6F4F6` (background), white `#FFFFFF`, coral accent `#F4845F`, deep navy `#173B4D`
- **Typography:** Clean geometric sans throughout; big friendly numerals for pricing
- **Imagery:** Bright modern surgery, genuine smiles, coastal light
