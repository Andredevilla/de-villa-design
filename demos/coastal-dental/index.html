About Coastal Dental

A practice that feels nothing like a dentist's.

We started Coastal Dental because we were tired of patients arriving anxious and leaving confused about what they'd just paid for. Our whole practice is built around the opposite experience — clear communication, honest recommendations, and a team that genuinely looks forward to seeing you.

  • Experienced team — 3 dentists and a dedicated oral health therapist, plus 4 support staff who know your name.
  • Transparent pricing — we quote before we treat. No hidden fees, no inflated estimates.
  • Flexible payments — HICAPS on the spot for all health funds, plus Afterpay and Zip interest-free plans.
  • Accessible location — ground floor, fully wheelchair accessible, with free on-site parking at Shop 3, Burleigh Heads.
  • Kid-friendly — gentle, patient care and Medicare CDBS bulk-billing for eligible children.
