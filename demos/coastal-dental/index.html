About Coastal Dental

A practice that feels nothing like a dentist's.

Our vision

We started Coastal Dental because we were tired of patients arriving anxious and leaving confused about what they'd just paid for. Our whole practice is built around the opposite — clear communication, honest recommendations, and a team that genuinely looks forward to seeing you.

What we offer

Everything your smile needs

From routine check-ups to full smile makeovers — gentle care for the whole family.

Check-up & Clean

A thorough exam, professional scale and polish, and digital X-rays to catch problems early — all in one friendly visit.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Whitening, porcelain veneers, and full smile makeovers tailored to your face. Results that look naturally brilliant.

Invisalign®

Dental Implants
