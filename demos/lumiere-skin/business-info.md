# Lumière Skin & Laser — Business Information
*(Fictional demo business created by De Villa Design as concept portfolio work.)*

## Identity
- **Name:** Lumière Skin & Laser
- **Slogan (header):** Science meets glow.
- **Subheader:** A doctor-led medical spa in South Yarra — honest skin advice, clinical-grade treatments, results you can see.
- **Voice:** Luxe but honest. Clinical authority softened with elegance. No before/after overpromising; "consult first" ethos.

## Services / Treatments
1. **Skin consultations** — doctor-led skin analysis with personalised plan (complimentary, 30 min — hero offer)
2. **Laser skin rejuvenation** — pigment, redness and texture (from $290/session)
3. **Anti-wrinkle & dermal fillers** — subtle, medically supervised (consult required)
4. **Medical-grade facials & peels** — from $180
5. **Laser hair removal** — all skin tones, from $89/area
6. **LED & skin-needling programs** — collagen support packages from $240

## Conversion hooks
- Complimentary 30-minute skin consultation — hero offer
