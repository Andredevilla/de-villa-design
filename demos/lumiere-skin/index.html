Our approach

Honest advice.
Clinical authority.

Lumière was founded on a simple belief: great skin results come from honest conversations, not hard sells. Every treatment plan begins with a thorough consultation — because the right treatment for your skin is the only treatment worth having.

Our approach is doctor-led and evidence-based. We use clinically validated technology and medical-grade formulations. All advertising and procedures comply with TGA guidelines; prescription treatments require an in-person consultation before commencement.

We believe in transparent, per-session pricing — and we will tell you honestly if a treatment may not be suitable for you.
