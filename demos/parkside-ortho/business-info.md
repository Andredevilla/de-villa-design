## About
A specialist orthodontic practice — braces and aligners are *all* we do, led by a registered specialist orthodontist (three extra years of training beyond a dentist). We treat kids, teens and a growing number of adults, with clear pricing, flexible interest-free payment plans, and a bright, low-stress clinic that takes the dread out of "the orthodontist".

Specialist orthodontist (AHPRA-registered) · interest-free payment plans · all health funds (orthodontic extras) · free initial smile assessment · early-treatment (interceptive) program · 3D digital scanning (no goopy impressions).
