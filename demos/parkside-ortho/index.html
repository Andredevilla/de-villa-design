Why Parkside

A specialist practice — not a dentist doing braces

A specialist orthodontist completes three extra years of training beyond a dentist — and braces and aligners are the only thing we do. We treat kids, teens and a growing number of adults, with clear pricing, flexible payment plans, and a clinic built to feel calm, not clinical.
