## About
A calm, light-filled osteopathy studio a few doors up from the Gertrude St cafés in Fitzroy. Treats the whole body, not just the spot that hurts — desk-bound necks, runners' hips, new-parent backs, and the colicky newborns who keep them up at night. Sessions are unhurried (30–45 min), and patients leave knowing what to do between visits.

HICAPS instant claiming · DVA & WorkCover accepted · pregnancy-friendly tables · infant & paediatric trained · online booking 24/7.
