# Serenity Physio — Business Information
*(Fictional demo business created by De Villa Design as concept portfolio work.)*

## Identity
- **Name:** Serenity Physio
- **Tagline (header):** Move freely again.
- **Subheader:** Expert physiotherapy in the heart of Melbourne — hands-on care that gets you back to the life you love.
- **Voice:** Calm, expert, reassuring. Clinical credibility with warmth.

## Services
1. **Musculoskeletal physiotherapy** — back, neck and joint pain assessment and treatment
2. **Sports injury rehab** — return-to-play programs for athletes of every level
3. **Post-surgical rehabilitation** — guided recovery after orthopaedic surgery
4. **Dry needling & massage** — targeted release for stubborn muscle tension
5. **Clinical Pilates** — equipment-based strength and mobility classes (small groups)
6. **Ergonomic & posture assessments** — workplace and WFH setups
