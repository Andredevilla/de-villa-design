What we treat

Our services

From acute injuries to long-term wellness — our team covers the full spectrum of physiotherapy care.

Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy

Assessment and treatment for back, neck and joint pain — finding the source, not just the symptom.

Sports Injury Rehab

Return-to-play programs designed for athletes at every level — from weekend warriors to elite competitors.

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Guided recovery after orthopaedic surgery, tailored to your surgeon's protocol and your personal goals.

Dry Needling & Massage

Targeted relief for stubborn muscle tension — combining dry needling and therapeutic massage techniques.

Clinical Pilates

Equipment-based strength and mobility classes in small groups — build a resilient body from the inside out.

Ergonomic & Posture Assessments

Comprehensive workplace and WFH setup reviews to prevent pain before it starts.

Our team

Physiotherapy the way it should be.

Serenity Physio was founded on a simple belief: that everyone deserves unhurried, expert hands-on care. Our team of four AHPRA-registered physiotherapists and one exercise physiologist brings genuine clinical depth — and they take the time to listen.

Whether you're recovering from surgery, managing a chronic condition, or chasing a personal best, we'll build a treatment plan that fits your life — not a one-size-fits-all protocol.
