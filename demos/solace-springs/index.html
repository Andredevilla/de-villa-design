The property

The Retreat

Tucked into the Daylesford hills, Solace Springs is a small property built around one idea: that restoration requires unhurried space and genuine quiet.

  • ◻ 12 forest suites — each with private outlook, stone bath, and hand-selected linen
  • ◻ 3 private bathing pavilions — bookable for exclusive use by guests
  • ◻ Mineral spring circuit — cold plunge, hot soak, steam grotto, and outdoor pool
  • ◻ Seasonal restaurant — set daily menu, all dietary requirements catered

  • ◻ Adults-only (16+) — respectful of the quiet we're all here for
